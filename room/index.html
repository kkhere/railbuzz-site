What this is

A chat room for the passengers on one train, on one day. Delays, platform changes, which coach is where, and whether the pantry has come round yet — from the people actually on board.

Rooms close shortly after the train completes its run. Everyone is verified by mobile number and appears under a generated alias, never their real name.

Opening it

Open RailBuzz, find this train, and tap Join Chat.

Tapping this link will open the room directly in a future release.
