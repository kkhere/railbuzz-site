Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026 · App: com.railbuzz.app · Beta release

RailBuzz shows live Indian Railways information and lets passengers on the same train talk to each other. This policy describes exactly what the app collects, why, and who it is shared with. It covers the RailBuzz Android app and nothing else.

What we collect

DataWhyKept
Mobile number Verifying you are a real person. Chat is the core of RailBuzz, and a verified number is what keeps passengers accountable for what they post. Until you delete your account
Name, and email if you give one Greeting you in the app. Both are private and never shown to other passengers. Until you delete your account
Passenger alias (e.g. Traveller-392) This is the only name other passengers see. It is generated for you, not chosen. Until you delete your account
Chat messages Delivering them to the other passengers in that train's room. Until you delete your account
Reports and blocks Keeping chat rooms safe. See deletion
Approximate location Finding your nearest station, and working out whether you are travelling on the train whose room you have opened. Not stored — see below
Usage events (screens opened, messages sent) Understanding which features are used during the beta. Unlinked from you on deletion
Crash reports Finding and fixing crashes. Up to 90 days

Location, specifically

We do not store your location, and we never send your coordinates to our servers.

Your position is used on your device to work out two things: the nearest railway station, and how far you are from the track of the train whose chat room you have opened. Only the result leaves the device, as a coarse range such as "2–10 km", never a latitude and longitude. There is no location history, and your city, village or address is never shown to other passengers.

Location is optional. If you decline the permission, everything else in the app continues to work; you will just need to pick your station yourself.

What other passengers can see

Only your passenger alias and the messages you post. Your mobile number, name, email and location are never shown to anyone else using the app.

Chat rooms are public to the passengers in them. Please do not post anything in a room that you would not want those people to read.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. We use these providers:

RailRadarTrain numbers and station codes only. No personal data is sent.
Google GeminiOnly what you type to the RailBuzz AI assistant. Ordinary passenger chat is never sent to it.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and data at any time, from the app menu. See Deleting your RailBuzz account.
  • Turn off location in Android settings. The app keeps working.
  • Reshuffle your alias from the profile screen.
  • Ask for a copy of your data by emailing us.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Access to your data is enforced at the database level, so one passenger cannot read another's profile, private details or reports. This is a beta release, and while we take security seriously, no system can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

Children

RailBuzz is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and notify you in the app.

Contact

Questions, data requests, or grievances under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act: support@railbuzz.in. We aim to respond within 30 days.
