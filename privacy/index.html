Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026 · App: com.railbuzz.app · Beta release

RailBuzz shows live Indian Railways information and lets passengers on the same train talk to each other. This policy describes exactly what the app collects, why, and who it is shared with. It covers the RailBuzz Android app and nothing else.

How long chat is kept

Who can see itHow long
Passengers in the roomUntil 15 minutes after the train completes its run
RailBuzz, for safety review3 days, then deleted
Messages that were reportedUp to 90 days

A train's chat room exists for that journey only. Once the train arrives the room closes for passengers, and the messages are deleted within three days.

Where a message has been reported, we keep a copy of it and of the surrounding conversation for up to 90 days, so the report can be reviewed properly and so we can respond to a lawful request from the police or a government authority. This is the only chat that outlives the three days, and it is deleted automatically once that period ends.

Deleting your account does not shorten the three days. Your messages disappear from every room immediately and nobody using RailBuzz can see them, but a copy only our safety team can reach is held for the same three days that already apply to everyone else, and then erased. Otherwise deleting an account would be the quickest way to destroy the evidence of harassing somebody. The same number also cannot register again during those three days. Full detail on the deletion page.

Location, specifically

We do not store your location, and we never send your coordinates to our servers.

Your position is used on your device to work out two things: the nearest railway station, and how far you are from the track of the train whose chat room you have opened. Only the result leaves the device, as a coarse range such as "2–10 km", never a latitude and longitude. There is no location history, and your city, village or address is never shown to other passengers.

Location is optional. If you decline the permission, everything else in the app continues to work; you will just need to pick your station yourself.

What other passengers can see

Only your passenger alias and the messages you post. Your mobile number, name, email and location are never shown to anyone else using the app.

Chat rooms are public to the passengers in them. Please do not post anything in a room that you would not want those people to read.

Automated safety checks on chat rooms

Chat rooms are monitored automatically for signs that a conversation has turned abusive — a spike in reports and blocks, several people turning on one person, or language associated with threats, sexual harassment, hate, scams or someone’s private details being posted.

Most of this happens inside our own database and nothing leaves it. When those signals suggest a room may have turned abusive, a recent extract of that room’s messages is sent to Google Gemini for a second opinion, because words alone cannot tell an argument from a threat. Before it is sent:

  • passenger names are replaced with placeholders (P1, P2, and so on)
  • phone numbers and email addresses are removed
  • no account identifier, train number or travel date is included

The result is only ever shown to our moderators. It never automatically removes a message, restricts an account or closes a room — a person decides that. We deliberately do not let an automated system silence a chat room, because a room may be the fastest way passengers have to warn each other about a real emergency.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and data at any time, from the app menu. See Deleting your RailBuzz account.
  • Turn off location in Android settings. The app keeps working.
  • Reshuffle your alias from the profile screen.
  • Ask for a copy of your data by emailing us.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act you also have the right to correct or complete inaccurate data, to have it erased, to withdraw your consent at any time, to grievance redressal, and to nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become incapacitated. To exercise any of them, write to the Grievance Officer named below.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Access to your data is enforced at the database level, so one passenger cannot read another's profile, private details or reports. This is a beta release, and while we take security seriously, no system can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

Children

RailBuzz is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. The app puts you in conversation with strangers, and we do not operate the parental-consent mechanisms that Indian law requires for users under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, email us and we will close it.

How long we keep an inactive account

If you do not use RailBuzz for three years, we will delete your account and personal data. We will email or notify you at least 48 hours beforehand, so you can open the app and keep it if you want to.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and notify you in the app.

Contact, and your right to complain

Our Grievance Officer, who is also our contact person for personal data, is Kiran Kumar — grievance@railbuzz.in.

We aim to respond substantively within 72 hours. The outer limits are 15 days for complaints about content or conduct (IT Rules, 2021) and 30 days for requests about your personal data (DPDP Rules, 2025).

If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India about a data matter, or appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee (gac.gov.in) about a content decision. Full detail, including what to include in a complaint, is on Grievance Redressal.
