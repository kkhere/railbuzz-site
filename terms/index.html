1. What RailBuzz is

RailBuzz shows Indian Railways train information and lets passengers on the same train talk to each other in a chat room for that journey.

RailBuzz is not an official Indian Railways channel. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for Indian Railways, IRCTC, or any government body. We do not sell tickets, and we cannot change your booking.

2. You must be 18 or older

RailBuzz is for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. The app puts you in conversation with strangers travelling on the same train, and we do not operate the parental-consent mechanisms that Indian law requires for users under 18.

If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will close it. If you believe a minor is using RailBuzz, email grievance@railbuzz.in.

3. Your account

  • You sign in with a mobile number, verified by a one-time code. One account per number.
  • Other passengers see only a generated alias such as Rider-A3F2. Your mobile number is never shown to them. Your name and email, if you give one, are never shown either.
  • Anonymity between passengers is not anonymity from us. We retain the account behind each message so that reports, sanctions and lawful requests mean something.
  • Keep your phone and your number secure. You are responsible for what is posted from your account.

5. Moderation, and what we can do

Passengers can report a message, and can block another passenger so they no longer see them. Reports are reviewed by a person. Automated checks decide what a human should look at — they do not decide what happens to anyone.

We may, in proportion to what happened:

  • remove a message;
  • pause your ability to post, usually for hours;
  • restrict your account for a longer period;
  • close your account permanently, for serious matters such as threats, doxxing, sexual content involving minors, or sustained harassment;
  • close a chat room to new messages.

Most restrictions lapse on their own. If you think a decision was wrong, you can appeal — see Grievance Redressal.

6. Train information is indicative

Timings, platforms, coach positions, PNR status and running status come from third-party sources and may be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Chat messages are passengers' opinions, not verified fact.

Do not rely on RailBuzz for a decision that matters — catching a connection, boarding, or anything involving safety. Check the official Indian Railways sources.

7. Asking for help in the app

RailBuzz lets you raise a request — a lost item, a medical problem, a platform change — to the other passengers in your train's chat room.

RailBuzz does not dispatch help. A request reaches the passengers in that one chat room and nobody else. No railway staff, no control room, no ambulance, no police. For an emergency, call Rail Madad 139, Emergency 112 or RPF 182.

Whether anybody responds is entirely up to the passengers who see it. We do not guarantee a response, and we are not responsible for what any passenger does or fails to do.

Retention is set out in our Privacy Policy: chat is visible to passengers until shortly after the journey ends, our safety team can see it for three days, and reported messages are kept for up to 90 days.
